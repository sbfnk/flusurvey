# Packages for analysing flusurvey data

Functions for reading, cleaning and linking the questionnaire responses
of FluSurvey, the UK online cohort survey of influenza-like illness, and
for estimating incidence and episodes of illness from them.

## Details

[`read_data`](https://sbfnk.github.io/flusurvey/reference/read_data.md)
reads the questionnaire files of one season,
[`extract_data`](https://sbfnk.github.io/flusurvey/reference/extract_data.md)
cleans and links the seasons with
[`merge_data`](https://sbfnk.github.io/flusurvey/reference/merge_data.md),
[`get_incidence`](https://sbfnk.github.io/flusurvey/reference/get_incidence.md)
estimates incidence and
[`bouts_of_illness`](https://sbfnk.github.io/flusurvey/reference/bouts_of_illness.md)
groups symptomatic reports into episodes.
